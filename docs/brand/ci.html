1. Who we are

The rule. Every surface says what MyThuso is, what GilbertOne is, and that neither is a live service yet — in the same words, because a product that describes itself three different ways has not decided what it is.

MyThuso

Nurse-led home healthcare for South Africa. A patient books a visit; a SANC-registered nurse comes to the house; a registered doctor reviews what the nurse found. Built by Akhanya IT Innovations, Johannesburg.

GilbertOne

GilbertOne is a short list of approved answers with a name. It matches what you type, or say on a phone, against that list and replies in sentences people at MyThuso wrote down in advance. GilbertOne is not a doctor, not a nurse and not a person.

GilbertOne is the product's name; Your Thuso AI Doctor is its descriptor, and the descriptor is never read without its disclosure: Your Thuso AI Doctor · not a person, and not a doctor. Powered by ThusoIQ: ThusoIQ is the name of MyThuso's clinical rules platform. In this build GilbertOne uses two things from it and nothing else: the approved sentences in this file and the emergency red flags Thuso SOS already asks about.

The honesty line every surface carries

The public page's notice bar, word for word from apps/web/src/features/Landing.tsx: MyThuso is in development. This page describes a service being built in Johannesburg. Nothing here books a visit, takes a payment or sends a nurse anywhere.

Inside the product the shell's badge reads Design preview · Fictional data (packages/catalog/locales.json, shell.previewBadge), and every screen that depends on something not yet connected renders that capability's own notice from packages/catalog/capabilities.json — for the connected kit: No device is connected. These readings are sample data and no instrument has been paired.

  • one-notice-per-screen. A screen renders the notice for the capability it depends on. Not three of them stacked, and not one it does not use.
  • the-notice-is-the-contract. No screen writes its own not-connected sentence. It names a capability and the sentence is rendered from this file.
  • connected-needs-evidence. A capability may be marked connected only when evidence names a file that exists and blockedBy is empty. The build refuses it otherwise.

From CLAUDE.md, the rule under all of these: "Nothing here is a real service. No visit is booked, no payment taken, no clinical decision issued, no device contacted. Every screen that could be mistaken for the real thing says so."

Every foreground on every surface, measured

WCAG 2.2 AA. 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text (18.66px bold or 24px and over) and for the visual boundary of a control or a focus indicator (SC 1.4.11). The focus indicator is two rings, not one: no single colour clears 3:1 against both a white card and a forest panel, and controls exist on both. A light inner ring carries the dark grounds and a dark outer ring carries the light ones, so whichever ground it lands on, one of the two is legible. Tying the ring to --amber, which also labels warnings, made the two pull against each other the moment the warning was darkened enough to be readable.

Nothing on a screen runs for ever except what is explicitly ambient and gated: the signal dot on a live icon, the public page's two loops (its particles and the badge's pulse). Every such animation sits behind [data-decor="on"] on the document element, so one control — the button labelled Pause motion in apps/web/src/components/MotionPause.tsx — stops all of it, which is what WCAG 2.2.2 asks for. A spinner that spins for as long as a wait lasts is refused outright: the loading arc turns once as it arrives and rests.

respectReducedMotion is not a suggestion and not a speed setting. Reduced motion REMOVES these animations rather than shortening them: a 420ms entrance run at 80ms is still a thing that moved. Web answers prefers-reduced-motion, iOS answers accessibilityReduceMotion, and Android follows the system animator duration scale — which reports 0 when a reader has turned animations off, so a duration multiplied by it is genuinely none.

Glass: A glass panel may not sit on top of text. Blur behind a word is the one place translucency stops being decoration and starts being a legibility problem. backdrop-filter is expensive on the mid-range Android handsets this product is actually for. Where it is unsupported — and inside @media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce) — glass resolves to the opaque floor colour, which is exactly the colour every contrast figure was measured against. The fallback is not a degradation; it is the measured case.
