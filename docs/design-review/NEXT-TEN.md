# Ten proposed features and visual improvements

These are proposals for a future round, separate from the implemented chart animations.

1. **Customizable Home:** choose, reorder and resize care widgets around personal priorities.
2. **Interactive chart comparisons:** compare available periods and tap a point for its exact date, value and units in a native detail sheet.
3. **Pre-visit check-in:** short, focused cards for concerns, questions and preparation before a visit.
4. **Medication routine:** reminders for prescribed schedules, with a clear daily checklist and completion ring.
5. **Document scanner:** native camera capture, crop and text recognition for results and referrals, with review before saving.
6. **Family care feed:** permission-based updates on appointments and shared care tasks, organised by person.
7. **Weekly care planner:** combine appointments, care tasks and reminders in a readable calendar.
8. **Private appointment widgets:** native Home/Lock Screen widgets showing the next confirmed visit, with sensitive details hidden by default.
9. **Editorial service pages:** warm photography, preparation steps, duration and clear pricing in a dedicated service detail screen.
10. **Calm evening theme:** deep forest surfaces, warm typography, clear contrast and reduced-motion support.

Suggested first priorities: customizable Home, chart comparisons and pre-visit check-in.
