# MyThuso — care that feels like home

The new direction starts from the supplied editorial fitness and wellness references. It replaces the former page composition with an oversized typographic opening, a broad cinematic photograph, and a separate citron pricing panel. The patient dashboard brings a photographic cover, dated readings, upcoming care and a sculpted lavender journal into an asymmetric grid.

## How the references are interpreted

- [Veggy](https://dribbble.com/shots/27550673-Veggy-AI-Nutrition-Recipe-App): botanical colour, warm human imagery and generous, rounded surfaces. MyThuso uses pale citron, olive ink and warm paper rather than adopting a food-app identity.
- [NEUROA](https://dribbble.com/shots/27507421-NEUROA-Cinematic-AI-Healthcare-App): cinematic contrast and editorial scale. The photographic care cover gives the app a clear focal point.
- [Lucid](https://dribbble.com/shots/27496566-Lucid-AI-Wellness-App-Concept): a quieter atmosphere for reflection. A decorative moon is drawn separately in CSS, SwiftUI and Compose. It does not imply a sleep or recovery score.
- [Sleep Quality dashboard](https://dribbble.com/shots/25901629-Sleep-Quality-Health-Dashboard): clearly separated readings, trends and activity. MyThuso shows its own Passport readings, real catalogue prices and current preview appointments.
- Supplied fitness website compositions: oversized headlines, compact navigation, deliberate changes in scale and photographic storytelling.

The logo is the supplied MyThuso logo. Its colours have not been changed. Native iOS and Android use their platform navigation, text scaling, image views and drawing APIs.
